# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People analyzing marketplace and social-media comments who need to classify sentiment and identify opinions by aspect.

## Product Purpose

SentimenLab provides sentiment and aspect-based analysis for individual comments and CSV batches, with tools to manage training data and model configuration. Success means users can understand sentiment results and the aspects behind them.

## Brand Personality

Clear, practical, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Noisy, generic dashboards that obscure the analysis.

## Design Principles

- Keep sentiment results and aspect-level explanations easy to scan.
- Make model choices and analysis workflows clear.
- Keep controls accessible and avoid relying on color alone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.1 AA, support reduced motion, and never rely on color alone.
